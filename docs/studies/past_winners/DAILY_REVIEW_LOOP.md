# Daily Review Loop for Past Winners

## Pre-Market (10-15 min)

1. Pick 2 winners from the same pattern family likely to appear today.
2. Review only these four items:
   - entry trigger quality,
   - invalidation quality,
   - risk:reward quality,
   - market context fit.
3. Write one if-then line:
   - "If today's setup matches X and Y, I execute. Else I pass."

## During Market Hours

- Compare live candidates with winner checklist.
- Do not force if R:R is poor or volume confirmation is missing.
- Use pre-defined position size; no emotional override.

## Post-Market (15 min)

1. Save charts for 1-3 names (before, breakout, exit or current state).
2. Add/update entries in `trade_data/past_winners/catalog.json`.
3. Log at least one lesson under "what worked" or "mistake".

## Weekly Deep Review (60 min)

- Count sample size by pattern family.
- Update in `trade_data/past_winners/glossary.json`:
  - win rate,
  - average R,
  - median hold days,
  - strongest market conditions,
  - frequent failure conditions.
- Remove low-quality pattern variants from your active playbook.

## Subconscious Conditioning Rules

- Repetition beats intensity: study every day, even small sessions.
- Study winners and failed lookalikes side by side.
- Always connect pattern shape to risk:reward and context.
- Focus on behavior, not stock names.
